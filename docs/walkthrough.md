# Walkthrough: APDCL Official UI Integration

I have completely transformed the application to perfectly match the official branding of the Assam Power Distribution Company Limited (APDCL).

## What Changed

### 1. Main Landing Page
- The generic lightning bolt icon has been fully replaced with the real APDCL logo image fetched directly from the official website.
- The header proportions and color blocks exactly match the blue and white styling of APDCL's corporate identity.

### 2. Login & Registration Page
- **Old Design**: A dark, "cyberpunk" theme with glowing neon borders.
- **New Design**: A clean, bright corporate portal. It now uses a white background with a clean card, matching the professional look of a government utility portal.
- Features the official APDCL logo at the top of the login forms.
- Replaced the indigo buttons with the official APDCL Orange (`#f89b1c`).

### 3. APDCL GENNIE Widget
- **Widget Icon**: The floating button in the bottom right corner is now the official APDCL Orange instead of a pink/orange gradient.
- **Header**: The chat window header has been changed to the official APDCL Blue (`#115599`).
- **Bot Avatar**: The AI now uses the real APDCL logo as its avatar picture instead of the Zap icon.
- **Chat Bubbles**: When the AI responds, it now uses clean white message bubbles. When you respond, it uses the APDCL blue.

### Verification
You can see all of these changes immediately by navigating around your application in the browser. 

👉 Open **[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)** and click the **"Consumer Login"** button at the top right to see the new portal!
